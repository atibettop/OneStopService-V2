SERVICE TERMS

Service terms

  • Minimum service term of 3 months.
  • An active position is a role currently in the recruitment process.
  • Qualified candidates are those who pass initial screening against the requirement.
  • Highly complex or specialised roles may carry an additional fee.
  • Premium job-board and advertising costs beyond the media support budget are charged at cost and require your prior approval.
  • Recruitment does not guarantee a candidate count or time-to-fill, as both depend on the labour market and each role's requirement.
  • Replacement-guarantee details and payment terms follow the service agreement.
