FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few questions?
Start here.

Talk to our team ↗
Who is payroll outsourcing for?+

For organizations looking for payroll support. Scope depends on headcount, pay cycles and data complexity.

What do we need to get started?+

Start with the services you need, headcount and a short brief. The team will advise on the next information needed.

How is payroll information handled?+

Access, transfer channels and scope are agreed before onboarding. Please do not send individual payroll data through the initial contact form.

How is recruitment priced?+

Monthly retainer and success-fee services are available. See the Recruitment page for pricing and conditions.

Can we combine several services?+

Select multiple services in the quotation form so the team can assess a combined scope.

Is a long-term contract required?+

Contract length and conditions depend on the service and scope, as set out in your proposal.
